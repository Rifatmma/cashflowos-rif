# Moving Walls brand rules (from the Visual Identity Guidelines)

These are baked into the engine — this file is for judgement calls and for explaining choices to the team.

## Personality
Explorer archetype · tagline "No Boundaries for delivering customer success" · uplifting, resilient, tech-centric, client-focused. End lines lean on **"No boundaries."**

## Colour
- Primary / secondary (large areas, backgrounds): `#2B3C92` blue, `#1B1E2B` navy, `#66C9F3` sky, `#BFE7FB` pale. Logo gradient runs sky → blue → navy → near-black.
- Font colour: `#273E8F` on light, white on dark.
- **One accent per video**, rotated between videos: Sunburst `#FEDA24 → #F59222`, Crimson Blend `#EE2E2D → #E03A36` or Emerald Wave `#6DDF6A → #4BEF6E`. The guidebook offers all three for small elements; that is exactly how the accent is used (heading chevrons, one emphasis line, strike-throughs, the "new" highlight, the shape behind a cut-out). On light backgrounds, accent text uses the deep tone of the same accent so it stays readable.
- Special-event colours (magenta / sunset / teal) only for event campaigns.

## Type
Poppins only. Title = Bold/ExtraBold, subtitle = SemiBold Italic, body = Regular. No serif display faces (that was the reference film's look, not ours).

## Graphic elements (vector, in `engine/shapes.js`)
- **MW zigzag icon** — solid and outline alternate; may be cropped at the frame edge ("breaking boundaries"); horizontal, vertical or slanted. Outline stroke thin.
- **Direction chevrons** — right-facing triple arrow = progress; used as heading markers (Sunburst) and as the chevron-push transition.
- **Connection dots** — network texture (`connection_dots_1..4.png`) = relationships/partnership.
- **Logo** — never recoloured except white-on-dark; never distorted; keep clear space. Full logo = mark + "moving walls" wordmark.

## Motion language (what makes it ours, not a copy)
Kept from the reference: cutting on the beat and the blur-to-focus text entrance — rhythm only.
Ours: zigzag sweeps, chevron pushes, iris, whip-slide and bar wipes as transitions; three openers (cover zigzag pattern, giant cropped word, huge MW mark); chevron section labels; drifting connection-dot textures; zigzag footer strip; flat geometric illustrations and data visuals in brand blues. With photos: black-and-white cut-outs over a flat accent shape with a giant cropped word behind, the editorial collage look from the team's reference reels. No film grain, no HUD/timecode/crop marks, no serif type.
