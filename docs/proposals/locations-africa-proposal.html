Outdoor Advertising in Africa

Africa’s Out-of-Home (OOH) advertising industry is expanding alongside rapid urban development and infrastructure investment. With over 40% of the population now living in urban areas (World Bank), cities are becoming increasingly dense, mobile, and commercially active, creating ideal conditions for outdoor advertising.

The continent’s young demographic - median age under 20 in many countries, combined with rising consumer spending and mobile connectivity, is driving demand for more dynamic and engaging advertising formats. Digital transformation is also accelerating, with growing adoption of Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) screens in key markets such as Nigeria, Kenya, and South Africa.

OOH Market Potential in Africa

Africa’s OOH sector is poised for strong growth, supported by urbanization, infrastructure expansion, and increasing advertiser interest in high-impact media. According to the World Bank, Africa’s urban population is expected to continue rising rapidly, with cities adding millions of new residents each year.

Major cities like Lagos, Cairo, Nairobi, and Johannesburg are already established advertising hubs, while secondary cities are emerging as new frontiers for brand expansion. Investments in roads, public transit, and airport infrastructure are opening up premium advertising inventory across the continent.

At the same time, the shift toward digital screens and programmatic capabilities is enabling advertisers to deliver more targeted, data-driven campaigns. As connectivity improves and urban economies expand, Africa presents a compelling opportunity for brands looking to scale their outdoor advertising presence.
