DOOH Market Landscape Qatar

Qatar has developed a highly connected OOH environment supported by rapid urban development, modern infrastructure, international tourism, major sporting events and a growing digital advertising ecosystem.

The country's population reached 3,214,609 at the end of December 2025, according to Qatar's National Planning Council. Population levels fluctuate throughout the year as the country's large expatriate and visitor populations move in and out of the country, making real-world audience movement an important consideration when planning OOH campaigns.

Doha remains at the centre of Qatar's OOH market. The city's concentration of business districts, residential areas, shopping malls, hotels, cultural attractions and transport infrastructure creates multiple opportunities for brands to reach audiences throughout their daily journeys.

The wider Doha metropolitan area extends this opportunity into destinations such as Lusail, Al Rayyan and The Pearl-Qatar, where residential, commercial, retail, hospitality and entertainment environments bring different audience groups together.

Qatar's international connectivity also creates opportunities to reach visitors. Hamad International Airport can accommodate more than 65 million passengers annually, while Qatar's tourism infrastructure continues to expand across hospitality, retail, entertainment, cultural and sporting destinations.

Digital Out-of-Home is becoming increasingly important as advertisers move from traditional static media towards dynamic, data-driven advertising. Digital screens can support flexible scheduling, contextual creative, audience-based planning and programmatic activation, allowing campaigns to respond more effectively to changing audience movements and environments.

Market research estimates indicate continued growth in Qatar's OOH and DOOH sector, with one industry analysis estimating the combined market at approximately US$41.84 million in 2025, rising to US$49.48 million by 2030.

For brands, this means OOH in Qatar is increasingly about more than buying a physical billboard. Audience intelligence, location data, contextual signals, dynamic creative and campaign measurement can help advertisers determine where, when and how to reach people in the real world.
