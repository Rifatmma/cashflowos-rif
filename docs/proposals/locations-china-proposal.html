Why Chinese Brands Are Expanding Globally

China is home to more than 1.4 billion people and one of the largest economies in the world. As businesses look beyond domestic growth, many are investing in international expansion across fast growing markets such as Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and as well as established markets such as Europe and the Americas.

International expansion presents significant opportunities, but it also introduces new challenges. Brands must navigate different media ecosystems, audience behaviours, market regulations, and campaign measurement standards while maintaining a consistent brand experience.

Digital Out-of-Home advertising enables brands to reach audiences in premium public environments while combining the impact of outdoor media with audience intelligence and measurable performance.

Why Add DOOH to Your International Marketing Strategy?

As Chinese brands expand into international markets, digital channels such as search, social media, and online video play an important role in driving engagement. Digital Out-of-Home (DOOH) complements these channels by extending brand visibility into the physical world, helping brands reach audiences across multiple touchpoints throughout their journey.
